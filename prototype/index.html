Frequently Asked Questions
Who is eligible for Senior Division (SD) and Senior Wing (SW) NCC at SBU Ranchi?
All regular 1st and 2nd year students of Sarala Birla University (B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Sc, Diploma) under 26 years of age who satisfy minimum physical standards (SD height 170cm, SW height 152cm, 1600m run) are eligible.
What are the career benefits of the NCC 'C' Certificate?
Holders of 'C' Certificate with Grade 'A' or 'B' receive direct 5-day SSB interview call letters for Indian Army (IMA/OTA), Navy, and Air Force under the NCC Special Entry Scheme without appearing for the UPSC CDSE written exam, plus bonus marks in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF/BSF/CRPF/CISF).
How does the 18-digit Application Number tracking work?
Upon submitting Form 1, candidates receive an authentic 18-digit Application ID (e.g. 192026102300018491) encoding battalion code '19', submission date, sequential queue index, and security checksum. This number tracks scrutiny, PET dates, and final enrolment.

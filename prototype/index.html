Direct SSB Entry Benefits

Holders of the NCC 'C' Certificate (Grade 'A' or 'B') receive direct 5-day SSB interview calls for Indian Army (IMA/OTA), Navy, and Air Force without appearing for CDSE exams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining 19 JHR BN NCC at Sarala Birla University.

What are the physical efficiency criteria for SD and SW?
For Senior Division (Male): Minimum height ~170 cm, 1.6 km run within 6 mins 30 secs, 20+ push-ups, 30+ sit-ups. For Senior Wing (Female): Minimum height ~152 cm, 800m run, and core flexibility tests.
How does the NCC Special Entry Scheme work for SSB?
Cadets who graduate with an NCC 'C' Certificate with Grade 'A' or 'B' are exempt from the UPSC CDSE written examination and receive direct 5-day SSB interview call letters for IMA Dehradun, OTA Chennai, and equivalent Naval/Air Force academies.
Is attendance in regular parades mandatory?
Yes. A minimum of 75% attendance in regular battalion training parades and completion of at least one Annual Training Camp (ATC/CATC) is mandatory to appear for the 'B' Certificate examination.
